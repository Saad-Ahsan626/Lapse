# Service logos

Third-party service logos bundled with Lapse. They are trademarks of their owners and are
used **only to identify** the service a user subscribes to.

## Rules

1. **Source:** official press / brand kit only. Never ripped from websites or icon packs.
2. **Unmodified:** no recolouring, redrawing, cropping into new shapes or merging with the
   Lapse mark. `ServiceTile` only adds padding and a white background.
3. **File name = catalog key:** `netflix.svg`, `spotify.svg`, `youtube_premium.svg`, …
   SVG preferred. If a service offers no SVG, use a PNG of 192px or more.
4. **Never** use these logos in the Play Store icon, screenshots or feature graphic, or in
   marketing. Use initials tiles there.
5. The About screen shows the disclaimer: *"All trademarks and logos belong to their
   respective owners. Lapse is not affiliated with or endorsed by them."*
6. Remove a logo promptly if its owner asks.

> This folder is added to `pubspec.yaml` in **Phase 2**, together with the catalog. This
> file is excluded from the bundle then (declare the logo files, not the folder, or move
> this file out).
